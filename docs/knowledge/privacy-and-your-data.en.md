# What Itqan stores, and who can see it

## Your documents

Your CV and transcript are stored on Itqan's own server. They are not sent to a
third-party service to be kept, and they are not shown to employers or to other users.

Only your own account can read them back.

You can delete any document from your profile at any time. Deleting removes the file
itself, not just the entry in the list.

Deleting a document does not erase results that have already been produced — those are
already recorded. It changes what a later re-run reads.

## Your account

Your email address belongs to your account. Itqan never overwrites it with an address
found inside your CV, so signing in always works with the address you chose.

New accounts confirm their email with a six-digit code. The code lasts ten minutes, and
five wrong attempts retire it — you can always ask for a new one.

## Your results

Your results — the roles you matched, your gaps, your recommended courses — are yours
alone. The assistant can only ever discuss your own results. It has no way to see another
person's, because the information it is given is built from your rows and nobody else's.

## Language

Itqan works in Arabic and English, and you can switch at any time. Your results do not
change when you do.
