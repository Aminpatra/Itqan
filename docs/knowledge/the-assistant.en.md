# Hud, the assistant

Hud is the assistant inside Itqan. It answers questions about your own results — how
ready you are, which roles matched and why, which skills you are missing, which courses
were recommended — and questions about Itqan itself, like this one.

## What Hud can and cannot do

Hud answers from two things: your own results, and Itqan's own documentation. If neither
covers your question, it says so. It does not fill the gap with a guess, and it will not
tell you about anyone else's results.

Hud cannot start anything on its own. It can suggest re-running your matching, but the
re-run only happens if you confirm it.

## Limits

- **30 messages a day.** The count resets at midnight, Muscat time.
- **One re-run a week.** It resets on Monday.

A re-run costs its credit whichever way you start it — from a conversation with Hud, or
from the documents screen in your profile — because it is the same work either way.

## Why the limits exist

Every message is a real request to a language model, and every re-run re-matches you
against the whole corpus. The limits keep that affordable, which is what keeps Itqan
free to use.
