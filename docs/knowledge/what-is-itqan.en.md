# What Itqan is

Itqan helps people in Oman work out which jobs they are ready for, which skills they are
missing, and which courses would close the gap.

You give it your CV. It reads it, compares what you can do against real job postings
collected from Omani sources, and tells you plainly where you stand: the roles that fit,
the skills those roles asked for that your documents do not yet show, and a course for
each of those skills where one exists.

It is built for graduates and job-seekers in Oman, and it works in Arabic and English.

## Who it is for

Anyone looking for work in Oman. It is most useful to someone early in their career, who
has qualifications and coursework but not much work history, because that is exactly the
person a normal job board serves badly: their CV does not yet say what they can do in the
words employers search for.

## What Itqan does not do

- **It does not apply for jobs on your behalf.** It shows you roles and links you to
  where they are posted. Applying is yours to do.
- **It does not promise anyone a job.** Nothing here is a prediction about whether you
  will be hired.
- **It does not show your documents to employers**, or to anyone else. See what Itqan
  stores.
- **It does not guess.** When something cannot be measured from your documents, Itqan
  says so instead of filling in a number. A blank in Itqan means "we do not know", never
  "zero".
