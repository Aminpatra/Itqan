# Understanding your results

## The readiness score

Readiness is the share of what your matched roles asked for that your documents already
evidence. If it reads 60, six in ten of the requirements across the roles you were
compared against are things you can already show.

It is **not** a grade, and it is not a prediction of whether you will be hired. It says
nothing about you as a candidate beyond what your documents happened to record.

If there was not enough in your documents to judge it, Itqan shows it as not measured.
It never shows zero in that case, because zero would read as "you match nothing", which
is a different and much harsher claim.

## Job matches

Each matched role carries the reason it matched: the actual skill of yours that satisfied
the requirement. A match with no such reasoning behind it is not shown at all, rather
than shown with a vague explanation.

Every posting links back to where it was published, so you can read the original and
apply there.

## Skill gaps

A gap is a skill the roles you matched asked for, that your documents do not evidence
yet. Next to each one, Itqan shows how many of your matched roles asked for it — that
count is the useful number, and it is not the same as the order the list is in.

A gap is not a criticism. It is frequently a skill you have and simply have not written
down anywhere a reader could see.

## Courses

For each gap, Itqan looks for one course that teaches it. Where nothing in the catalogue
covers a skill, it says so rather than offering something unrelated.

Courses are chosen so that the fewest courses cover the most gaps, and are ranked using
the provider's own ratings — weighted so that a perfect score from a handful of reviews
does not outrank a strong score from thousands.

## Liking and hiding

You can hide a job or a course you are not interested in. Hidden items stop appearing in
your lists and on your dashboard. Changing your mind brings them back.

## Re-running

Re-running matches you again against the postings open today, using the profile you
already confirmed. It is worth doing when time has passed, or after you add a document.
